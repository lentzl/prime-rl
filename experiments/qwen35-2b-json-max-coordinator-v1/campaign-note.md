# JSON-max coordinator campaign

The active intervention trains an e33-descended root coordinator while H176 remains
fixed. Selection uses executable direct and two-shard development outcomes, reports
child numerical and lifecycle evidence separately, and preserves sealed confirmation
data for one final selected checkpoint only.

The eight-update tiny diagnostic is complete. Although optimization was finite and
loss fell, the candidate scored 0/8 direct and 0/8 composed on its paired TRAIN-fit
slice. Target inspection confirmed that the correct `max(mapping.values())` operation
and final response are inside the assistant loss mask and within the 16k context.

The single allowed bounded exposure retry started fresh from e33 at learning rate
1e-6. Runtime accounting showed that 16 packed updates consume about one 572-row
corpus pass; the completed 48-update checkpoint therefore represents three passes,
not the one pass suggested by the naive 572/12 row calculation. Evaluate that
checkpoint immediately on paired TRAIN-fit, fresh development, and root retention.
At most 16 additional updates may reach the four-pass ceiling, and only if executable
behavior moves and root rehearsal is retained. Because the first run saved weights
only, this prospective final pass starts from its step-48 weights with a fresh AdamW
state at 5e-7 and uses the next 128 untouched TRAIN identities. The old 192-update
ceiling was never executed. C2C, recurrence, simulators, cross-resource composition,
worker retraining, and the parked H-ITER/T0 lanes remain out of scope.

## Terminal result

The bounded recipe is stopped after the allowed 16-update continuation. The main
curve consumed exactly 64 optimizer updates and 12,582,912 packed tokens (48 updates
at 1e-6, then 16 updates at 5e-7); the separate tiny diagnostic consumed 8 updates.
The final checkpoint is
`coordinator-json-max-curve-step48-continuation-lr5e7-pass4-v1/weights/step_16`,
with `model.safetensors` SHA-256
`3d622ca675111c6207052ffd47755e315fc235a64778a4edfa5e1caf7514ce76`.
Its 16-update loss moved from 0.054763 to 0.034677 with finite gradients, but loss
convergence did not solve the composed task.

On 32 untouched `valid_gen` identities at offset 32, the checkpoint solved 18/32
direct JSON-max tasks and 0/32 two-shard composed tasks. Children returned the exact
remote maximum on all 15 observed report attempts, while only 7/32 episodes completed
the strict child lifecycle. In the clearest near-misses the child result arrives, but
the root never reads the coordinator-owned JSON and emits `local_max: 0`; other runs
stop after saying that they are waiting. This local-compute-plus-fan-in transition is
the limiting behavior. Repeating the same transcript-style SFT recipe is therefore
not justified.

The exact six-task historical retention battery passed 6/6 cleanly: 2/2 direct,
2/2 flat, and 2/2 hierarchical. Its summary SHA-256 is
`6dd860f4a6b322c9009c319414c42e41ac78831ef8cf64a0ab6204623760551a`.
H176 remained fixed at
`77980e247bbccd6463ddda02cd42d2c357e15f8ec1ad0ea84627e008a8674a1e`.

Decision: retain the checkpoint as a useful non-promoted diagnostic—direct utility
and prior document coordination are real, but the target composed utility is absent.
The active recipe has zero training budget remaining. Do not select, promote, or run
sealed confirmation. Any successor should change the learning signal around the
root's local-work-before-yield and post-child fan-in decision, and should first prove
that transition on a tiny executable test rather than add more epochs or ceremony.
